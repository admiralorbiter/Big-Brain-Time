Requirements Change Control

Managing modifications to agreed-upon baselines. Requires Impact Analysis (evaluating cost, schedule, and scope footprint) and explicit authorization before committing changes to production baselines.

6.2 Requirements Change Control

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Plan-based lifecycles require explicit change control workflows:

  1. Change Request Submission;
  2. Impact Analysis (examining cost, schedule, and architecture consequences);
  3. Change Control Board (CCB) decision (accept, reject, defer);
  4. Stakeholder Notification & Tracking to closure.

Agile contrast: Change management happens implicitly. Change requests become backlog items, accepted when prioritized into a sprint.
