Requirements Process Quality and Improvement

Assessing and improving the requirements engineering process to align with quality standards (ISO/IEC, CMMI), process benchmarking, and security/CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) planning.

7.6 Requirements Process Quality and Improvement

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Key dimensions of requirements process quality:

  • Requirements process coverage by process improvement models (CMMI);
  • Process measurement metrics and benchmarking against industry baselines;
  • Improvement planning and execution;
  • Security / CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) process planning.
