⚡ Quick Reference Card Model Execution

Simulation and Execution

Executing or hand-interpreting formally specified models (e.g. running state machine simulations) to demonstrate behavior scenarios to non-technical stakeholders in place of static document reading.

5.2 Simulation and Execution

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Non-technical stakeholders may hesitate to inspect lengthy text documents. Formally specified model-based requirements allow engineers to "execute" or simulate the specification against concrete demonstration scenarios, convincing stakeholders that policies and processes are completely captured.
