Requirements Sources

Requirements originate from stakeholder classes (people/groups involved, affected, or influential) and non-person sources (legacy systems, defect databases, business policies, standards, and computing environments).

2.1 Requirements Sources

Canonical SWEBOK Text

A stakeholder is any person, group, or organization that is actively involved in the project, affected by its outcome, or able to influence its outcome.

Key Stakeholder Classes:

  • Clients: Those who pay for the software (organizational management).
  • Customers: Those who decide whether a product will be put into service.
  • Users: Those who interact directly/indirectly (broken down into distinct user classes by privilege, skill, and frequency).
  • SMEs & Operations: Subject matter experts, site reliability engineers, operations staff.
  • Support & Regulators: First-level support staff, regulatory agencies, professional bodies.
  • Negatively Affected Parties: People who can be negatively impacted if the project succeeds.

Non-Person Sources:

  • Legacy system documentation, mission statements, concepts of operations.
  • Interfacing systems, legacy defect databases, and computing infrastructure.
