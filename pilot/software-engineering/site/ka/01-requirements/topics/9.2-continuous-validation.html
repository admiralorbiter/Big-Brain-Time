Continuous Discovery & Runtime Validation

Extends requirements validation beyond pre-release document reviews into continuous production validation using the RIGHT Model, feature flags, telemetry, Service-Level Objectives (SLOs), and automated rollback/kill criteria.

9.2 Continuous Discovery & Runtime Validation

The RIGHT Model Pipeline

In modern continuous delivery environments (IEEE 2675 DevOps), requirements are explicit product hypotheses validated in production:

  • Explicit Hypotheses: Formulating requirements as testable business/user hypotheses.
  • Minimum Viable Experiments: Building targeted feature spikes or dark launches.
  • Feature Flags & Staged Rollouts: Incremental exposure to subset user cohorts (1% → 10% → 100%).
  • Outcome & Guardrail Metrics: Monitoring success indicators alongside system health guardrails.
  • Service Level Objectives (SLOs): Defining acceptable operational thresholds (error budget).
  • Rollback & Kill Criteria: Automated circuit breakers that disable features if guardrails fail.
