Economics of Quality of Service Constraints

QoS requirements (capacity, latency, reliability) evaluated through economic curves: identifying the Perfection Point, Fail Point, and Step-Function Cost Curve to find the most cost-effective performance target.

3.2 Economics of Quality of Service Constraints

Canonical SWEBOK Text

QoS constraints are often specified arbitrarily. Economic analysis models stakeholder value vs performance level:

1. Perfection Point

The most favorable level of performance beyond which additional capacity yields zero extra value (e.g. supporting more users than total world population).

2. Fail Point

The least favorable level of performance below which there is no further reduction in benefit and the system ceases to be viable.

Step-Function Cost Curves

Delivery cost increases as a step-function: an initial investment level enables performance up to a maximum plateau; further investment triggers step-ups to higher plateaus. The optimal requirement target maximizes Value(Level) - Cost(Level).
