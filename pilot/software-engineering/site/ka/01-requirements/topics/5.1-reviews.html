Requirements Reviews

Inspecting specifications to find omissions, invalid assumptions, and ambiguities. Effective validation requires three distinct reviewer perspectives (Business Stakeholders, Requirements Specialists, and Downstream Architects/Engineers).

5.1 Requirements Reviews

Canonical SWEBOK Text

The most common way to validate is by reviewing or inspecting a requirements document from 3 mandatory perspectives:

  1. Clients, Customers & Users: Check that wants and needs are completely and accurately represented.
  2. Requirements Specialists: Check that the document is unambiguous, precise, and conforms to standards.
  3. Downstream Architects & Developers: Check that specifications are sufficient to support design, architecture, and construction.

Checklists, quality criteria, or "Definition of Done" templates guide reviewers to focus on specific validation properties.
