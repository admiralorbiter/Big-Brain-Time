Scope Matching

Ensuring that the scope of requirements to architect, design, and construct does not exceed project budget, schedule, or staffing constraints. Where possible, scope matching should be quantitative (Functional Size Measurement units or Story Points).

6.3 Scope Matching

Canonical SWEBOK Text

When requirement scope exceeds cost, schedule, or staffing capacity, teams must negotiate:

  1. Reduce scope (remove lowest-priority requirements);
  2. Increase capacity (extend schedule or increase budget/staffing);
  3. A negotiated combination of both.

Agile lifecycle context: Velocity-based sprint planning automatically enforces scope matching by admitting only work that can reasonably be completed during the sprint iteration.
