Derived Requirements

Requirements not imposed by external stakeholders, but induced internally by development choices (e.g. architectural style decisions). What is a design decision to the overall project acts as a requirement to component sub-teams.

Derived Requirements Internal Constraints

1.10 Derived Requirements

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Requirements are context-sensitive and depend heavily on perspective:

"The aerospace industry has long used the term derived requirement to mean a requirement that was not made by an external stakeholder, but was imposed inside the larger development team."

Example: An architect chooses a pipes-and-filters architecture. To external project stakeholders, this choice is a design decision. But to the sub-team building a specific filter component, that choice is a binding requirement.
