Sociotechnical & Affected-Party Analysis

Expanding stakeholder analysis beyond paying clients and end-users to systematically identify structural power imbalances, non-user data subjects, ethical value elicitation (IEEE 7000-2021), and harm modeling.

9.5 Sociotechnical & Power Analysis

Extended Stakeholder & Ethical Taxonomy
  • Direct Users & Customers: Primary system operators and purchasing decision makers.
  • Data Subjects: Individuals whose personal data or behavior is processed by the software without direct interaction.
  • Affected Non-Users & Communities: Third parties impacted by system decisions (e.g., algorithmic credit scoring or routing decisions).
  • Regulators & Adversaries: Compliance enforcement bodies and bad-actor threat vectors.
  • Representation & Power Record: Documenting who participates, who is represented by proxies, and who lacks decision power.
  • IEEE 7000 Value Elicitation: Translating human, social, and ethical values into traceable system requirements.
