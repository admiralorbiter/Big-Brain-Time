Measuring Requirements (FSM & Story Points)

Evaluating requirements volume using Functional Size Measurement (FSM) or Story Points to estimate project effort, cost, maintenance complexity, and defect density metrics.

7.5 Measuring Requirements

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Evaluating requirements volume and specification quality:

  • Functional Size Measurement (FSM): Evaluates functional requirements size (IFPUG, NESMA, COSMIC standards).
  • Story Points: Relative measure of requirement size and complexity in Agile planning.
  • Quality Indicators: Measure clarity, completeness, and consistency of the requirements specification document as a whole.
