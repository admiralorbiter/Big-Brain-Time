Nonfunctional Requirements

Nonfunctional requirements constrain the technologies to be used in the implementation or specify acceptable performance levels. They divide into Technology Constraints and Quality of Service (QoS) Constraints.

Technology Constraints QoS Constraints

1.5 Nonfunctional Requirements

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Nonfunctional requirements constrain automation implementation:

  • What computing platforms or database engines?
  • How accurate do results need to be? How quickly must results be presented?
  • How many records of a certain type need to be stored?

They have essential relationships among themselves — modifying one nonfunctional requirement (e.g. optimizing speed) frequently impacts others (e.g. code modifiability or maintainability).
