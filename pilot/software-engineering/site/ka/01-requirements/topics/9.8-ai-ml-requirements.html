AI, Data, and Generative AI Requirements Primer

Traditional software directly specifies deterministic logic. Machine learning and Generative AI behavior depend on training data distributions, prompt grounding, evaluation splits, and probabilistic abstention bounds (NIST AI RMF 1.0, ISO/IEC 42001:2023).

9.8 AI, Data, and Generative AI Requirements Primer

Core AI/ML Requirements Categories
  • Data Provenance & Consent: Licensing, representativeness, data minimization, and contamination prevention.
  • Subgroup & Intersectional Evaluation: Evaluating model performance across distinct demographic/operational slices rather than relying on a single aggregate accuracy metric.
  • Uncertainty & Human Escalation: Specifying confidence thresholds, abstention logic, and human-in-the-loop fallback.
  • Drift & Retraining Triggers: Continuous monitoring bounds for covariate/concept drift in production (NIST AI RMF 1.0).
  • Generative AI Specifics: Grounding requirements, hallucination boundaries, prompt injection resilience, red-teaming test suites, and output verification filters.
  • Documentation Artifacts: Datasheets for Datasets (Gebru) and Model Cards for Model Reporting (Mitchell).
