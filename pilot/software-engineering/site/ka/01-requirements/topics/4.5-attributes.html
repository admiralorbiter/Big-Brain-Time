4.5 Additional Attributes of Requirements

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Key supplemental attributes for requirements tracking:

  • Unique Tag: Supports forward and backward tracing.
  • Rationale: Explains why the requirement is important.
  • Source: Role or name of stakeholder imposing the requirement.
  • Type: Classification (Functional, QoS, Technology Constraint).
  • Priority & Stability: Value/Dissatisfaction ranking & volatility assessment.
  • Change History: Versioned configuration management log.

Gilb's Planguage Attributes

Planning Language (Planguage) defines quantitative attributes: Scale, Meter, Minimum, Target, Outstanding, Past, Trend, and Record.
