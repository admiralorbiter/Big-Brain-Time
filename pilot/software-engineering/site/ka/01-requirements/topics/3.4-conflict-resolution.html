3.4 Addressing Conflict in Requirements

Canonical SWEBOK Text

As stakeholder diversity grows, requirement conflicts become inevitable. SWEBOK outlines 2 primary strategies:

1. Principled Negotiation (Consensus Resolution)

Software engineers must avoid unilateral decisions. Consulting stakeholders to reach consensus ensures decisions remain contractually traceable back to customer authority (Fisher & Ury, Getting to Yes).

2. Product Line Engineering (Invariant vs Variant)

Partition requirements into Invariant requirements (agreed upon by all stakeholders) and Variant requirements (where conflict exists). Software is designed with design to invariants for common core logic and design for change (customization points) for variants (e.g. Celsius vs Fahrenheit units in weather apps).
