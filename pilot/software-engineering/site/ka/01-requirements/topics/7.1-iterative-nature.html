⚡ Quick Reference Card Breadth vs Depth

Iterative Nature of the Requirements Process

Requirements work must balance breadth-wise knowledge (discovering the full boundary of system capabilities) with depth-wise specification (detailed policy & scenario rules). It is highly unlikely that requirements work can be completed in a single pass.

7.1 Iterative Nature of the Requirements Process

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Requirements for typical software have both wide breadth and significant depth:

"The tension created by simultaneous breadth-wise and depth-wise requirements in real-world projects prompts teams to perform requirements activities iteratively. At some points, elicitation and analysis favor expanding breadth; at other points, expanding depth is called for."
