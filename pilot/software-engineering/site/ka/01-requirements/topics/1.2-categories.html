Categories Breakdown

Requirements are partitioned into Software Product Requirements (form, fit, function) and Software Project Requirements (cost, schedule, process constraints). Product requirements further divide into Functional and Nonfunctional (Technology vs QoS).

1.2 Categories of Software Requirements

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Requirements categorization structures how requirements are elicited, analyzed, specified, and validated. SWEBOK establishes a formal taxonomy hierarchy:

Software Requirements
├── Software Project Requirements (Process, Cost, Schedule)
└── Software Product Requirements
    ├── Functional Requirements (Policies & Processes)
    └── Nonfunctional Requirements
        ├── Technology Constraints (Platforms, DBs, Languages)
        └── Quality of Service Constraints (Performance, Security, Availability)
