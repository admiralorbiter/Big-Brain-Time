Section 04 Overview

Requirements Specification

Record requirements so they can be remembered and communicated across time. Covers natural language formats, acceptance criteria (ATDD/BDD), model-based specifications (UML/SysML), requirement attributes, and incremental vs comprehensive baselines.

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Requirements specification records requirements for memory and communication. Downstream maintainers should not be able to discern the lifecycle used in earlier development from the form of requirements alone.
