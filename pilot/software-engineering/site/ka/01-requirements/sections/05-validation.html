Section 05 Overview

Requirements Validation

Gain confidence that requirements represent true stakeholder needs as currently understood. Three primary techniques: Multi-perspective Reviews, Simulation & Execution of formal models, and Exploratory Prototyping.

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Requirements validation confirms that requirements are understandable, consistent, complete, and conform to standards.
