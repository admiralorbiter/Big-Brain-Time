Section 09 Overview

Modern Companion Layer

Contemporary extensions, standards updates, sociotechnical analyses, and AI/ML practices extending the classical SWEBOK v4.0 baseline.

Companion Perspective

Rather than replacing classical SWEBOK principles, Section 09 provides a modern companion layer treating requirements as: hypotheses requiring experimentation, sociotechnical obligations, continuous runtime metrics, assurance graphs, and AI/data-dependent behavior specifications.
