Section 02 Overview

Requirements Elicitation

Surface candidate requirements through stakeholder analysis, workshops, story mapping, observation, and non-person sources to minimize incompleteness.

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Elicitation (also called capture, discovery, or acquisition) aims to surface candidate requirements. Elicitation is not a passive activity — engineers must actively draw out tacit knowledge that stakeholders leave unstated or cannot easily express.
