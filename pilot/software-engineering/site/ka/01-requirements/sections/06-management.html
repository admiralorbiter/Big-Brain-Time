Section 06 Overview

Requirements Management Activities

"Maintaining the agreement on what software is to be constructed over time." Covers Requirements Scrubbing, Change Control processes, and quantitative Scope Matching.

Canonical SWEBOK Text

Requirements Development is "reaching agreement on what software to build." Requirements Management is "maintaining that agreement over time" as scope, priorities, and business environments evolve.
