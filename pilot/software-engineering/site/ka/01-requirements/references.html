Reference Library

Recommended Reading & Standards

Comprehensive list of foundational standards, empirical research papers, continuous delivery frameworks, and AI governance guidelines cited across the SWEBOK KA 01 wiki.

1. Core Requirements Practice & Standards

  • ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018: Systems and software engineering — Life cycle processes — Requirements engineering.
  • ISO/IEC 25010:2023: Systems and software engineering — Systems and software Quality Requirements and Evaluation (SQuaRE) — Product quality model (9 characteristics).
  • IIBA BABOK v3 (2015): A Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (Hierarchy: Business, Stakeholder, Solution, Transition).
  • Mavin et al. (2009): "Easy Approach to Requirements Syntax (EARS)," 17th IEEE International Requirements Engineering Conference.
  • Adzic, G. (2011): Specification by Example: How Successful Teams Deliver the Right Software. Manning Publications.
  • SEI Quality Attribute Workshop (QAW): Quality Attribute Scenarios (Source, Stimulus, Artifact, Environment, Response, Measure).

2. Continuous Delivery & Operational Requirements

  • IEEE Std 2675-2021: IEEE Standard for DevOps: Building Reliable and Secure Systems Including Application Build and Deployment.
  • Fagerholm et al. (2017): "The RIGHT Model for Continuous Experimentation," Journal of Systems and Software.
  • Reinertsen, D. G. (2009): The Principles of Product Development Flow: Second Generation Lean Product Development. CASSANDRA.

3. High-Assurance, Safety & Ethics

  • IEEE Std 7000-2021: IEEE Standard Model Process for Addressing Ethical Concerns During System Design.
  • ISO/IEC/IEEE 15026-2:2022: Systems and software engineering — Systems and software assurance — Part 2: Assurance case.
  • GSN Community Standard v3 (2021): Goal Structuring Notation Standard. SCSC.
  • W3C WCAG 2.2 (2023): Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.
  • NIST SSDF 1.1 (2022): Secure Software Development Framework (SP 800-218).
  • Leveson, N. (2012): Engineering a Safer World: Systems Thinking Applied to Safety. MIT Press.

4. Data, AI & Machine Learning Requirements

  • NIST AI RMF 1.0 (2023): Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework.
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023: Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system.
  • Gebru et al. (2021): "Datasheets for Datasets," Communications of the ACM.
  • Mitchell et al. (2019): "Model Cards for Model Reporting," Proc. FAT* '19.
  • Amershi et al. (2019): "Software Engineering for Machine Learning: A Case Study," IEEE/ACM 41st ICSE.
  • ISO/IEC 21031:2024: Information technology — Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) specification.
