✅ What This Dataset CAN Answer

  • Casualty Burden: Which corridors, intersections, and hours suffer the highest volume of injury and fatal crashes in Kansas City.
  • Conditional Fatality Drivers: Among injury/fatal crashes, how darkness, pedestrian involvement, single-vehicle status, and vehicle count relate to fatality odds.
  • Temporal Concentration: Hourly and weekday distribution of crashes vs fatality rates.
  • Hotspot Persistence: Year-to-year rank stability of high-crash corridors.

❌ What This Dataset CANNOT Answer (Dataset Boundaries)

  • Traffic Exposure / Risk Rates: Without Annual Average Daily Traffic (AADT) denominators, a major interstate like I-435 ranks top by burden simply because millions of vehicles travel it.
  • Overall Crash Rate: Property-damage-only crashes are omitted from this export; overall crash probability cannot be calculated.
  • Causal Factors (Speed, Alcohol, Weather): Speeding, impairment, and NOAA weather measurements are not present in this 15-column dataset.
  • 2024 Reporting Format Shift: Missouri adopted a updated crash reporting standard starting in 2024; minor trend variations across 2023–2024 should be interpreted with reporting format updates in mind.
