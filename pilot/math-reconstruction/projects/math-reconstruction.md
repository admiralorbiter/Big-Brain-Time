# Mathematics Reconstruction Lab

**Purpose:** Rebuild a rigorous calculus-to-analysis foundation through textbook study, computational experiments, and selective Lean formalization, while producing a public set of notes, examples, and reflections on the learning process.

## Accepted Rationale & Decisions

- **Focus on Rigor First:** Prioritize clear delta-epsilon proofs and foundational axioms before moving to multivariable concepts.
- **Formalization Boundary:** Formalize key theorems (e.g. Intermediate Value Theorem, Bolzano-Weierstrass) in Lean 4 only after pen-and-paper proofs are thoroughly understood.
- **Artifact Output:** Maintain structured Markdown notes under `notes/` and worked exercise solutions under `exercises/`.

## Core Scope

1. Real Number Axioms & Completeness
2. Limits of Sequences & Continuity
3. Differentiability & Mean Value Theorems
4. Riemann Integration & Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
5. Pointwise & Uniform Convergence
